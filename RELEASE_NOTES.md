Athanor 0.7.0: medicines.

- Personal Genome now reads 19 genes that affect how people respond to medicines, using the guidelines of the Clinical Pharmacogenetics Implementation Consortium (CPIC): CYP2C19, CYP2C9, CYP2D6, DPYD, TPMT, NUDT15, SLCO1B1, UGT1A1, G6PD, CYP3A5, NAT2 and others.
- A summary card lists what CPIC recommends for each of its well-supported medicines given your results. It is something to show a doctor or pharmacist, never a reason to start, stop or change a medicine.
- Each gene card says how far the call can be trusted from the positions your file has, checked against 1000 Genomes people, and says plainly when your file cannot settle the gene. Rare variants are flagged as needing a clinical test.
- The reference data has a new version; the Personal Genome tab offers the update.
- Your file is still read only on this computer and never uploaded.
